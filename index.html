About the Project

This project focuses on providing affordable water filtration, promoting proper sanitation, and teaching safe hygiene practices to reduce waterborne diseases in underserved communities.

Our Solutions

Portable Filters

Simple, durable filters that remove contaminants and make water safe to drink.

Sanitation Training

Community workshops on handwashing, latrine use and safe water storage.

Local Partnerships

Working with schools, clinics and community leaders to ensure long-term adoption.

Expected Impact

  • Fewer waterborne illnesses
  • Improved school attendance
  • Stronger community resilience
